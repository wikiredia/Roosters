The **tab bar**: Today, Rooster, Chat, Me (a custom bar with the brand's active pill). Signed out → welcome. An invite opened before signing in is accepted here once signed in.

## `TABS`, `TabBar`

Tab icons and labels, and the bar itself (accessible as a tablist). The Chat tab carries a rose badge with your
unread messages (`useChats`, asked every 15 s; screen readers hear the number too).

## `TabsLayout`

The tabs (switching cross-fades) and the pending-invite check.
