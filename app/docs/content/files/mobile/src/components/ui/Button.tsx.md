Buttons in the design system: primary, secondary, ghost, ink, onYou; sizes; loading state. They scale down
a touch while pressed (`Motion.usePressScale`).

## `IconButton`

A round icon-only button (close, back). It always needs a label for screen readers. `filled`: on a round surface
with a border; `solid`: the icon itself filled (the friends list's thumbtack of a pinned friend).

## `Props`, `Button`

A labelled button with an optional icon.
