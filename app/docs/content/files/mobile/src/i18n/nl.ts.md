**Dutch texts: the source of truth for keys.** Short, plain sentences; say 'je', never 'u'. Grouped by screen (`welcome.`, `auth.` for the sign-in screen after 'Create an account' / 'I already have an account', `found.` for 'you already had an account' / 'no account yet', `login.`, `details.` for lesson details (field names and the onboarding step), `google.` for what can go wrong with Google sign-in, `school.`, `steps.` for Today's 'complete these steps', `pushStep.` for the notifications step in onboarding, …).

## `Key`

The union of all keys (other languages must have every one).

## `nl`

All Dutch texts. Pinned friends: `friends.pinned` / `friends.others` (the friends list's cards), `friend.pin*` and
`friend.pinned` (the toggle, the thumbtack's labels, the marker). Experiments in Ik: `me.labsHead`, `me.weekDrag`,
`me.weekDragSub` (the sub line says it's a test that may change or go away).

## `nlOne`

Singular forms for `n = 1` ('1 klas').
