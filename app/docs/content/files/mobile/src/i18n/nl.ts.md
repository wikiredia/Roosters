**Dutch texts: the source of truth for keys.** Short, plain sentences; say 'je', never 'u'. Grouped by screen (`welcome.`, `login.`, `google.` for what can go wrong with Google sign-in, `school.`, `steps.` for Today's 'complete these steps', `pushStep.` for the notifications step in onboarding, …).

## `Key`

The union of all keys (other languages must have every one).

## `nl`

All Dutch texts.

## `nlOne`

Singular forms for `n = 1` ('1 klas').
