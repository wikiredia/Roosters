**Classes you skip** (`skipped_lessons`, written by `r_me.skip`): one row per course, day and stretch (a double
period skipped at once is one row). `schedule.lessons` marks the lessons in it `skipped`, so they don't make you
busy: for your breaks and for your friends'. Friends never see that you skipped. Exported with your data and deleted
with the account (`accounts._OWNED`).
