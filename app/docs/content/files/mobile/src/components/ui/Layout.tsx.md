Layout building blocks: cards, list rows, section headings.

## `Card`, `TogetherCard`

A white card with a hairline border; the yellow 'together' card.

## `RowProps`, `Row`

A list row: at least 64 high, a title and a sub line, optional left (avatar/icon) and right (value/button), chevron.

## `SectionHead`, `Section`, `ListHead`, `Divider`

Section title rows, spacing, the small uppercase heading, a divider.

## `TogetherIcon`

The yellow circle with the cup that marks 'together'.
