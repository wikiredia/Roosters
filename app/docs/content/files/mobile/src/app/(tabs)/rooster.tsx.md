**Rooster** (timetable): day mode (1–3 days side by side with shared breaks in yellow) or week mode, with a day picker that skips weekends. Tapping a lesson opens `LessonSheet`.

## `DayColumns`

Several days from the cached Today answers, with friends' shared breaks.

## `DayPicker`

'Mo 19 · Tu 20 …' with arrows to the previous/next week.

## `Mode`, `schoolDay`

Day or week; stepping Monday–Friday only.

## `RoosterScreen`

The tab.

## `T`

Type of `t()`.

## `Week`

Your week (`WeekView`).
