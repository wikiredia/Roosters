**Line icons** from the design boards: 24 grid, 1.5 stroke, round caps, drawn with react-native-svg. Never emoji.

## `IconName`, `Props`, `Icon`

Names, props, and the component (colour is a design token). `undo` (the arrow curling left) is the Rooster tab's
'Terug'; `coffee` marks a free moment in the timetable (`together`, a cup too, is for shared breaks). Chat: `send`,
`more` (the "…" for a chat's info), `flag` (report) and `block`.
