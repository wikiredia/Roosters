Tap on a lesson: when, what, where, and who else is free if it's cancelled.

## `OverlapSheet`

'Welke les?': the lessons of a tapped overlap in time order (kind colour, time, kind, room). Picking one closes it
and the screen opens that lesson's `LessonSheet`.

## `Props`, `LessonSheet`

A bottom sheet. Keeps the last lesson while it slides away (`lesson` becomes null when it closes).
